## Immediate conclusions

1. The largest mismatch is component architecture and state presentation, not the absence of data
   features.
2. A selected-topic toolbar is a defining Xmind behavior and must be implemented in X39.
3. The inspector needs object-aware sections and compact rows, not another color patch.
4. Export and presentation already have substantial functionality but expose different hierarchy and
   control density.
5. Dynamic Outliner evidence is now captured. Drag-hold, dark appearance and matched DPI pairs remain.
